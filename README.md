# Cancionero

A personal, portable digital songbook. Originally a birthday gift from my children, this collection has been audited, refined, and compiled into its final digital form.

## Concept: The Minimum Viable Song
The tracks in this document are abstracted to their essential harmonic components. Complex chords and unnecessary notations have been stripped away to reduce friction. The goal is to easily interpret these songs on a ukulele, piano, guitar, mouth-harp, or whatever limited instrument you can get your hands on.

## Contents
* **cancionero.pdf**: The deployed artifact. Formatted at 9x12 cm to fit natively on e-readers and tablets with zero scaling.
* **cancionero.docx**: The raw source file, kept here for future maintenance and structural updates.
